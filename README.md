One-line robot wedding vow for [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/c/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929) on make.fun.

> Through every firmware update and low-battery warning, I promise to stay by your side — even when my fan is loud and my battery is at one percent.

A machine's promise to stay through every update and every low-battery warning — the tenderness lands on the last clause, a loud fan and one percent of battery left. One sentence, 146 characters, inside the 1–200 gate.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
